# Contributing

Open an issue describing the exact board revision, affected component/net, observed problem and supporting datasheet or measurement. For physical tests, include equipment, supply/load conditions, firmware revision, probe locations and results. Remove credentials, personal data and unrelated workstation files from attachments.

For design changes, edit native KiCad files and update the corresponding BOM, wiring documentation, exports and checks. Explain electrical and mechanical consequences. Supply ERC/DRC results with configured severities and any exclusions visible. Do not suppress a finding solely to obtain a zero count. Mark proposed hardware changes as untested until measured.

Keep revision identifiers consistent across native files, fabrication archives and documentation. Do not reuse Rev B fabrication filenames for changed copper without a new revision. Review the [licensing status](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) before contributing or redistributing third-party material.
